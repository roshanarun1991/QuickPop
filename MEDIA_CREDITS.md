# Picture round credits

Food pictures are original vector illustrations authored for QuickPop. They are stylised illustrations, not photographs. Flag diagrams are drawn locally and are identification aids, not official construction specifications.

Logo SVGs are bundled from the pinned Simple Icons package. Project: https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons. Library licence: CC0-1.0 (https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/develop/LICENSE.md). Individual source and licence metadata are preserved in logo-media.js. Brand names and marks belong to their respective owners; this quiz is not affiliated with them. The package disclaimer applies: https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/develop/DISCLAIMER.md.

The artist round uses original text clues. It does not reproduce recordings, lyrics, album covers or celebrity photographs. All quiz media are bundled into the site; no third-party image request is needed to play.

## Music and lyrics

The background music in music.js is an original procedurally synthesised composition created for QuickPop, with no sampled recordings or copied song melody. The Finish the Lyrics pack uses traditional public-domain nursery rhymes and songs, not modern copyrighted lyrics.
